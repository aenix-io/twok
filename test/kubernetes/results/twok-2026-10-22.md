Raw records of the runs after the fix of PR #14 (the agent reads the arbiter and the WAL while its etcd does not answer), summarised in [docs/testing.md](../../../docs/testing.md#twok-results).

Only the repeated Cilium deny verdicts are cut short. No secrets appear in the records.

## 10. One-way partition, HTTP arbiter, etcd-0 leader

The configuration that failed in runs 5 and 9. The pair was restored during the partition, through the connections the one-way cut leaves open. The harness of this run still counted every successful put on the loser during the partition as a second writer and reported FAIL; all 37 of those puts started after the loser had rejoined the survivor's cluster as a learner (+33.3 s; the first returned at +52.1 s, with the promotion at +52.2 s), so they went to the same cluster, and every one of them is present at the end. The check now leaves out puts after the member's rejoin, as in runs 11 and 12.
